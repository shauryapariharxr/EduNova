About EduNova

We build the courses we wished we'd had.

EduNova started as a frustration with courses that promised everything and delivered a certificate nobody checked. So we built something narrower on purpose — fewer courses, more finishing, mentors who actually reply.

Our story

Started by people who dropped out of three other course platforms first

In 2023, a small group of engineers and designers kept noticing the same pattern: they'd enroll, watch two videos, and quietly stop. Not because the content was bad — because nothing held them accountable, and no one was there when they got stuck.

EduNova is built around that gap. Every track pairs recorded lessons with a live mentor, a cohort of peers on the same schedule, and a project you actually have to ship to get certified.
