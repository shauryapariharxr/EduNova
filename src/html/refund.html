Refund Policy

Straightforward terms for when a course isn't the right fit.

On this page Overview 7-day refund window Eligibility How to request Non-refundable cases Subscription cancellations Contact us

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Overview

We want you to enroll in a track because it's genuinely useful to you, not because you feel locked in. This policy explains when and how you can get a refund if a course turns out not to be the right fit.

7-day refund window

You can request a full refund within 7 days of your purchase date, provided you meet the eligibility requirements below. Refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.

Eligibility requirements

  • The request is made within 7 days of the original purchase date.
  • You have completed less than 20% of the course content.
  • You have not already downloaded or claimed a certificate for the course.
  • The course was purchased directly through EduNova, not through a third-party bundle or promotional partner.

How to request a refund

Email support@edunova.com with your order confirmation and the course name, or use the contact form on our Contact page and select "Billing & refunds" as the subject. Our team typically responds within one business day.

Non-refundable cases

Refunds are not available once you've completed 20% or more of a course, claimed a certificate, or if more than 7 days have passed since purchase. Live mentor session slots that have already taken place are also non-refundable, since mentor time has already been allocated.

Subscription cancellations

If you're on a recurring subscription plan, you can cancel anytime from your account settings — this stops future billing but does not retroactively refund the current billing period. You'll retain access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Contact us

Questions about a specific order? Reach out at support@edunova.com or through the Contact page and we'll help you sort it out.
