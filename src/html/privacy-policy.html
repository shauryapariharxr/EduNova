Introduction

This policy explains what personal data EduNova collects when you use our platform, why we collect it, and the choices you have over it. By creating an account, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Course activity: lessons completed, quiz scores, time spent learning, and certificates earned.
  • Payment information: processed by our third-party payment provider — we never store your full card details on our own servers.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device type, and browser information, collected automatically to help us improve the platform.

How we use your information

We use your data to run and improve EduNova: delivering course content, tracking your progress, issuing certificates, responding to support requests, and sending you course-related updates. We do not use your learning data to sell you unrelated products.

Cookies & tracking

We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how people use the platform in aggregate. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features (like staying logged in) may stop working properly.

Data sharing & third parties

We don't sell your personal data. We share the minimum necessary information with trusted service providers who help us operate the platform — for example, our payment processor and email delivery service — each bound by their own confidentiality obligations.

Data security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, though no online system can guarantee absolute security. If we ever experience a data breach affecting your account, we'll notify you promptly.

Your rights

You can access, update, or delete your personal data at any time from your account settings, or by emailing us directly. You can also request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll notify you by email or through a notice on the platform before the changes take effect.

Contact us

Questions about your data or this policy? Reach out at support@edunova.com or through the Contact page.
